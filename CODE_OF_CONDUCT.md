# Code of Conduct

## The Short Version

Be decent. Sedim is built in the open, for developers who want to spend less time on solved problems. That only works if the community around it is worth participating in.

Harassment, discrimination, or deliberately hostile behavior has no place here - in issues, PRs, Discord, or anywhere else this project exists.

---

## Standards

Behavior that makes this community worth being in:

- Giving honest, constructive feedback and receiving it the same way
- Assuming good intent before assuming bad
- Helping people who are newer to the codebase, not gatekeeping
- Disagreeing on technical grounds without making it personal
- Acknowledging when you're wrong and moving on

Behavior that isn't acceptable:

- Harassment of any kind - public or private
- Discriminatory language or jokes based on identity, background, or experience level 
- Dismissing contributors because of how they write, where they're from, or what they don't know yet
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent
- Sustained bad-faith behavior after being asked to stop

---

## Scope

This applies anywhere the project exists 
: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, Discord, and anywhere someone is acting as a representative of Sedim.

---

## Enforcement

If something happens that shouldn't, report it to **hello@sedim.dev**. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. The identity of the reporter stays private.

Depending on the situation, responses range from a private conversation to a temporary or permanent removal from the community. The response fits the behavior => minor things get addressed directly, patterns of serious behavior get escalated accordingly.
